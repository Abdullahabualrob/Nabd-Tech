About the Project

A student research project at the intersection of computer engineering, artificial intelligence, and healthcare technology.

Nabd Tech proposes an AIoT-based system for continuous electrocardiogram (ECG) monitoring. The system aims to collect ECG signals using a low-cost sensing device and analyze them using deep learning techniques to investigate the identification of cardiac rhythm patterns.


A key focus is comparing Edge AI, Cloud AI, and Hybrid AI architectures to understand how the location of AI inference affects prediction performance, response time, resource usage, communication overhead, and reliability under changing network conditions.

Our Research Goals

The project aims to conduct a fair, patient-independent evaluation of different AI deployment architectures using consistent data splits and evaluation methods. The objective is to understand the trade-offs between predictive quality, real-time responsiveness, computational resources, and resilience to network delays or packet loss.

Research and Innovation

Nabd Tech explores how intelligent computing and connected devices may contribute to future cardiac monitoring research. The project is intended for academic investigation and further development.
